The steward skill (`.claude/skills/steward/SKILL.md`) no longer tells a merging session to squash with the pull-request title alone. Merge-boundary conditions set by a review now come first. A session must collect every condition that review records and reconciled dispositions put on the merge, including ones carried forward from earlier cycles: a required squash-message text or references, or a required merge commit. It must honour them over the default, and must not merge if one cannot be met. The merge message must also quote every `HOLD` the latest reconciled disposition carries forward, so the `HOLD` survives in `main`'s history. The cycle-3 assurance review of #1231 found (C3-A-10) that the previous default, followed literally, would have broken that pull request's F-09 condition: the squash message must name the A1 restore and reference #1225 and #1232.
