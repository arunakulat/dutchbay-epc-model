- **Constraint/exclusion buildable-area mask** (`analytics/gis/exclusion_mask.py`, #833) —
  build a Boolean buildable-area siting mask (`True` == a turbine may be sited) from
  config-first inputs: per-class setback buffers (`shapely.buffer` around dwelling/road/
  grid point/line features by their own distances), protected-area/wetland exclusion
  polygons, and a slope-threshold mask (`slope > slope_max`) from the #829 DEM slope layer
  (array or GeoTIFF). The exclusions are unioned and the buildable mask is their complement,
  optionally clipped to a site-boundary polygon (reuses `analytics.gis.boundary_clip` when
  present, else an in-array clip). Rasterised to a Boolean GeoTIFF + `.prov.json` + a
  `DataLake_Manifest_All.json` entry via the #20 exporter helpers, CASPER-guarded on the
  `[gis]` extra (rasterio + shapely). Diagnostics record per-constraint excluded-cell
  counts and the buildable fraction. KPI-neutral, add-only, not wired into the finance
  pipeline.
