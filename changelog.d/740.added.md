- Opt-in capital-structure optimizers (#740): a new `analytics.capital_structure_optimizer_v14` module with two analysis optimizers that complement the engine's single-lever gearing solve. `optimize_debt_mix` searches the debt tranche MIX (the lkr/usd/dfi split under `Financing_Terms.mix`; two free proportions on a deterministic 2-simplex lattice, USD absorbing the residual) to maximize/minimize an objective KPI (e.g. `equity_irr`, or minimize a WACC proxy) SUBJECT TO covenant constraints (`min_dscr_period`/`llcr`/`plcr` floors, a `balloon_pct` ceiling, ...). `optimize_capex_contingency` searches the capex CONTINGENCY fraction (a multiplicative uplift on a declared base capex, driving `capex.usd_total`) against the same objective/constraints. Both score every candidate through the canonical `analytics.evaluation_v14.evaluate_with_overrides` gateway (CCCDIR/ARCH-04 — no re-implemented pipeline, no redefined IRR/NPV), are deterministic (fixed grid, no stochastic sampling — MRM-01), and fail loud on an infeasible search: they return a structured `CapitalStructureOptimizationResult` with `feasible=false` + an `infeasible_reason` naming the binding covenant(s), never a bare crash or a silently-empty best. New result contracts in `analytics.contracts_v14`: `DebtTrancheMix`, `CapitalStructurePoint`, `CovenantConstraint`, `CapitalStructureOptimizationResult`. New opt-in Hydra CLI `analytics/cli/cli_capital_structure_optimize_hydra.py` (+ `conf/cli_capital_structure_optimize.yaml`). These optimizers are ANALYSIS tools invoked explicitly and are NEVER wired into `run_v14_pipeline`, so committed scenarios stay byte-identical (KPI oracle unchanged; the lender case project IRR remains `0.014551597740253388`). The rich per-candidate infeasibility diagnostics + full optimization audit log are the follow-up #741; #740 ships the optimizers plus a minimal result surface.
