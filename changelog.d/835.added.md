- **Area -> optimized-layout micro-siting tool** (`wind_resource.layout_optimizer`, #835, Deliverable A) — a DTU TopFarm optimizer built directly on the existing PyWake wake model that takes a **boundary polygon** (+ optional **exclusion zones**), a **wind rose**, a **turbine geometry** (rotor/hub/power-Ct) and a **minimum spacing** (in rotor diameters) and **generates** an AEP-maximising candidate layout respecting the boundary, min-spacing and exclusion constraints (`XYBoundaryConstraint` inclusion/exclusion zones + `SpacingConstraint`, `EasyScipyOptimizeDriver('SLSQP')`, optional `smart_start` seeding). It reports the optimized x/y, the optimized AEP, and the AEP uplift vs a **caller-supplied baseline** (e.g. the committed T1-T15). TopFarm is a new **opt-in** `[micrositing]` extra (NOT in `[wind]` or the CI toolchain), CASPER-guarded via `_require_topfarm`; its tests skip when the extra is absent. **KPI-neutral and add-only** — a pure library tool that only *proposes* a candidate; feeding a layout into the headline (Deliverable B) is KPI-moving and explicitly out of scope here.
