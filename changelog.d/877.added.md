- **Solar PV grid-capability plug-in (current-limited grid-following model)** (`analytics/grid/capabilities/solar.py`, `analytics/grid/capabilities/__init__.py`, #877 D4c) — the first per-technology grid-capability plug-in, answering the converter-interface capability question for a solar block: given its DC nameplate, DC:AC ratio (ILR) and grid-code reactive envelope, what fault-current and reactive (PQ) capability does the plant present at the point of connection? Solar PV is modelled as a **CURRENT-LIMITED, GRID-FOLLOWING** source — a fleet of PLL-tracked VSC inverters that ride a firmware over-current ceiling (~1.1 pu of rated current, `DEFAULT_FAULT_CURRENT_PU`) with **negligible fault infeed**. The load-bearing modelling rule: solar MUST NOT be modelled as a synchronous machine / voltage source — doing so would inject a spurious fault MVA at the POC and OVERSTATE the short-circuit ratio (SCR) the D1 screen exists to protect. `SolarFaultContribution` therefore reports the small current-limited infeed with `is_synchronous=False` so a downstream SCR aggregation credits it as ~0, not as a machine. **DC:AC clipping without double-counting the inverter loss**: the AC export rating (the reactive-capability base) is the pure geometric `dc_capacity_mw / dc_ac_ratio` cap (`ac_rating_from_dc`); the inverter efficiency + midday-clip energy loss is NOT re-applied here — it already lives in the `solar_resource.pv_producer` pvlib pipeline (`pvlib.inverter.pvwatts(...).clip(upper=ac_nameplate_w)`), and re-applying it would double-count it. The reactive/current capability is emitted as the D3 advisory `ReactiveCapabilityResult` (`method="closed_form_solar"`): a closed-form Mvar-headroom screen over the grid-code `pf_range`, reusing the D3 reactive math (`reactive_screen.pf_to_qp_ratio`) — a tighter grid-code pf demand than the inverter Q-envelope surfaces a positive Mvar shortfall (the STATCOM/MSC sizing figure). The plug-in is STANDALONE (keyed on the `finance.tech_types` classification via `is_solar_capability_tech`; a later dolphin wires dispatch) and reuses the D4a ride-through core + the D3 `ReactiveCapabilityResult` rather than modelling new dynamics. CASPER: the capability math is pure Python — no `pandapower`/`andes` import — so the default (grid-free) install covers 100% of the module. NO SPURIOUS PASS: a missing/ill-formed DC rating, ILR or grid-code pf_range raises (CESSPIT), never a defaulted pass. The screen is ADVISORY (`bankable=False`) and never feeds the finance engine, so committed-scenario KPIs are byte-identical (project IRR 0.014551597740253388 / equity IRR −0.05841298678542661 / min DSCR 1.285740985294611 unchanged). The `[grid]`-gated interop tests (real pandapower reactive load-flow + real ANDES LVRT solve seeded from a solar block's grid-code envelope) run in the opt-in `-m grid` lane with the `[grid]` extra installed.
