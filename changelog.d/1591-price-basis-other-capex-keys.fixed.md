Capex price dates: when a scenario declares `capex.price_basis`, the price-year factor now applies to every capex amount the engine reads, not only `capex.usd_total`. That covers `finance.capex_usd` and `finance.capex_total_usd`, the other `capex` and `costs` total aliases, the numeric `capex.breakdown` line items (with or without QRA contingency), `capex.lkr_total`, the tax bases `tax.depreciable_capex_lkr` and `tax.dep_base_lkr`, and the top-level aliases. Before, these amounts skipped the price date silently, so debt, NPV and tax could sit on different price years. The factor is applied automatically, as the owner ruled on 2026-11-14, and always disclosed: each key and its stated amount is listed under `capex.price_basis_applied.automatically_escalated`, a warning is logged, and the pipeline result gains a `capex_price_basis` block, present only when this happened. No committed scenario combines the block with these keys, so no committed result changes (#1591 review residual D-1/A-1, under #1578).
