- **Wind-rose speed enrichment** (`analytics.wind.wind_rose.build_wind_rose`, #826) — optional co-indexed `ws_series` adds calm-exclusion (`calm_limit`, default 2.0 m/s met convention), per-sector mean speed, an energy rose (per-sector share of `fᵢ·⟨vᵢ³⟩`, normalised to 1), and a sectorwise Weibull `(A, k)` fit (`scipy weibull_min`, `floc=0`; sparse sectors report `None`). KPI-neutral: the direction-only path is byte-identical when no speeds are supplied, and this remains display/provenance only — not wired into the wake loss or AEP.
