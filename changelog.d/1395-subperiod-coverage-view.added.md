Add a sub-period coverage view (`finance.subperiod_coverage_v14`, #1395 part c): debt service, coverage CFADS and DSCR per sub-period of a sub-annual grid, beside the annual figures. Scheduled service follows a new `debt_service` event-calendar kind and the bridge period's service lands in year 1; an undeclared one is spread evenly and flagged as assumed, because instalment dates are not yet evidenced (#920), and neither debt kind may be declared as `profile`. Each sub-period DSCR covers its instalment over its calculation period, the cash earned since the previous instalment. A sub-period is an instalment only where the placement gives it weight; the rounding residual closes on the last such sub-period, so no residual becomes a phantom instalment. Each year re-aggregates to the debt engine and reproduces `dscr_by_year`; interest on a balloon carried past maturity is read from the debt result and shown per sub-period but, as in `dscr_by_year`, kept out of the DSCR. The headline min DSCR and debt sizing do not change, no KPI moves, and F-5 bars every sub-period DSCR from a covenant, lender or IC representation.
