Offtaker receivables lag, switched off (#1893, dolphin 2a). A scenario may declare `cashflow.monthly.receivable_days` (a whole number, 0-365). The annual row builders then deduct the year's change in month-end receivables from CFADS after the risk haircut, so the debt sizing, covenant tests and waterfall see cash as collected. EBITDA and tax do not move, and the last balance is collected in the final month. The monthly cascade computes the change month by month, and the quarterly event calendar keeps it in its CFADS re-derivation. No committed scenario sets the key, so no KPI moves; turning it on for the lender case is a separate R3 change.
