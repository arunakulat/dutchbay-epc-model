- **Grid D7 — SCR-coupled harmonics/flicker + frequency-response headroom screen** (`analytics/grid/harmonics.py` + `analytics/grid/frequency_response.py`, #883) — the design-stage POWER-QUALITY screen at the POC, explicitly COUPLED to the D1 SCR/grid-strength result. It estimates the per-order harmonic VOLTAGE distortion (`V_h = |Zbus(h)| · I_h` from an OEM/config current-emission spectrum, referred to the plant-base fundamental impedance `1/SCR`), the total harmonic voltage distortion, and the injected current TDD, comparing each to the IEEE 519:2022 voltage limits (by POC voltage band) and the current-TDD limit INDEXED by the `Isc/IL` (= SCR) ratio; and the IEC 61400-21 short-term flicker `Pst = c · S_rated / Ssc` at the POC. Both the harmonic voltage and the flicker WORSEN as the SCR (equivalently `Ssc`) drops — the verdict DEGRADES on a weaker grid. When the `[grid]` extra is present the per-order `|Zbus(h)|` is taken from a pandapower frequency-domain bus-impedance scan (the collector-cable capacitance can RESONATE with the line reactance above the inductive `h·X1` estimate); when absent a closed-form inductive estimate is used. A light `frequency_response.py` sizes the primary-frequency-response de-load / droop headroom (MW held back) and the annual ENERGY foregone from the grid-code frequency ride-through band. Emits a new frozen `HarmonicComplianceResult` (CCCDIR: added to `contracts_v14`), with per-domain SCREENING verdicts that return an honest `None` (NOT-RUN) — never a spurious pass — when the emission spectrum / flicker coefficient is absent. These are SCR-coupled screening APPROXIMATIONS that degrade as SCR falls, NOT a standalone pass/fail: a true study is a frequency-domain PSS/E or PowerFactory harmonic scan against the utility's confidential base case with the OEM emission spectra. CASPER: pandapower is guarded at call-time (base install never imports it; closed-form fallback). Advisory / default-off / KPI-neutral — never feeds the finance engine, committed-scenario KPIs byte-identical.
