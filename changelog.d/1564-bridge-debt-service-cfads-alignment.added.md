Add config-first bridge period treatment options to the equity cashflow waterfall (Issue #1564): resolves the structural asymmetry where Year-1 operating debt service bears 1.5 periods of debt service (operating year 1 plus the pre-COD bridge half-year) against only 1.0 period of CFADS, triggering artificial sponsor shortfall cash calls under the post-#1452 shortfall model across 11 scenarios. The new configuration key `finance.debt.bridge_period_treatment` (or `Financing_Terms.bridge_period_treatment`) supports `historical_fold` (default, preserving exact byte-identical baseline scenario outputs), `equity_cfads_match` (Option A: credits matching bridge CFADS of 0.5 * year-1 CFADS to year 1 equity cashflow, curing synthetic shortfalls), and `construction_capitalized` (Option B: capitalizes bridge debt service as pre-COD financing cost). Enforced at pre-flight via `validate_config_for_v14` and documented in `docs/finance/BRIDGE_DEBT_SERVICE_AND_CFADS_EQUITY_WATERFALL_1564.md`.
