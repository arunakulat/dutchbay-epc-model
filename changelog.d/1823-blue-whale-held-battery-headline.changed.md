Nothing is restated. The step runs once per evaluation in the canonical pipeline, the reload gateway (`evaluated_scenario_config`) and the batch surface, and records what it added under `storage_options.headline_option_applied`. Only a held option can be carried, the turbine plus the battery must fit the block's `export_limit_mw`, and a native-currency `capex.input` or a capex without `usd_total` is refused.
