- Tools that read capex outside the pipeline no longer miss a declared capex price date
  (#1899, the #1591 follow-up). `finance.capex_price_basis.refuse_unapplied_price_basis`
  raises `UnappliedCapexPriceBasisError`, a `ValueError`, when a resolver is handed a
  configuration whose `capex.price_basis` has not been applied. It is called by the debt,
  KPI, EPC, equity-distribution and three-statement capex resolvers, `ReturnsConfig.from_yaml`,
  the FX and tenor screening, the BESS LCOS resolver and the solar assessment. The multi-tech
  WBS lets the refusal through instead of treating it as missing capex. The guard matches the
  key in any letter case, so a mis-cased declaration that the engine would not apply is
  refused rather than read as undated.
- The tools that re-load a scenario now read it through
  `analytics.evaluation_v14.evaluated_scenario_config`: `run_v14_pipeline_with_analytics`
  (except a native-capex scenario, whose returns block stays omitted, DOM-1645-R4-03), the FX
  and tenor screening (`run_fx_tenor_screening_for_scenario` and the Blue Whale loader) and the
  solar assessment's deterministic analyses. The solar assessment's Monte Carlo starts from the
  scenario as written, so each trial's pipeline escalates a capex draw from the price-year
  range. The Monte Carlo dry run applies the price date as each trial does. The capex Monte
  Carlo still perturbs the stated capex, which the pipeline escalates, and reports its capex
  percentiles and base as each trial's financed capex: the escalated capex plus a declared
  headline battery (#1823).
- Engine KPIs do not change. Reported figures move on the two dated Blue Whale scenarios
  (`blue_whale_wind_lot5b_2026`, `blue_whale_solar_bess_lot5b_2026`), so `VERSION` is 15.34.0
  under DOC-02:
  - Returns analytics (wind): capex USD 10,899,980 to 11,518,867.5; project IRR 16.34% to
    15.15%; project NPV USD 4,265,405 to 3,646,517; project MIRR 11.83% to 11.52%; equity IRR
    18.28% to 17.62%; equity NPV USD 2,439,297 to 2,253,631; equity MIRR 14.85% to 14.57%.
  - Returns analytics (solar + BESS): capex USD 4,501,220 to 4,962,595; project NPV
    USD -3,872,417 to -4,333,792; project MIRR 0.44% to -0.04%; equity NPV USD -1,264,198 to
    -1,402,611; equity MIRR 1.27% to 0.81% (IRRs undefined on both).
  - Solar assessment: the capex sweep's capex axis and the realised gearing in the gearing
    and DSCR-target curves are now on the financed capex; on the wind scenario the sweep's
    IRR, NPV and minimum DSCR also move at every factor other than 1.00, because the factor
    now scales the headline battery too.
  - The capex Monte Carlo's capex percentiles and base rise to the financed capex.
  No committed output reads these paths.
- A census test (`tests/finance/test_capex_price_basis_readers_1899.py`) classifies every
  module outside `tests/` whose source names `usd_total` or a quoted `breakdown` key. A new
  module that names either fails until it is classified; a reader that reaches capex by
  another key is not caught by it.
