The solar assessment report's gearing and DSCR-target optimisations now rank on equity NPV, not equity IRR (#1456, #1522 assurance A-3). On the report's competitive case, the DSCR-target optimum moves from 1.60x (equity IRR) to 1.05x (equity NPV); the gearing sweep has no feasible point under either measure.
