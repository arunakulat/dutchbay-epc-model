  The tax depreciation base now reads the same capex resolver as debt sizing and NPV, so a capex stated only under an alias (`finance.capex_usd`, `capex.total_capex` and the others) is depreciated instead of booking no depreciation; a stated capex that does not resolve is refused, and the toy capex fallback is never depreciated. Explicit `tax.depreciable_capex_lkr` and `tax.dep_base_lkr` keep their precedence. No committed scenario changes: a sweep of all 48 committed scenarios through the v14 gateway and the direct cashflow path gives identical results.
