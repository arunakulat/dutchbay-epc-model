## Notes

- **Duplicates / versions:** the Dutch Bay EIA exists in many forms (Sep-2025 final, June-2025 EN/Ta/Si drafts, partially-cleaned Word drafts) — keep the **Sep-2025 final + annexures** as canonical. `Environment impact of a wind turbine project.pdf` has 6 copies; several `(1)` duplicates appear. The `SPPA final draft 100 pages` and `Kollonnawa PSSREIP-5B 8-volume` sets are each **one document split across files** — index once, not per page/volume.
- **Code vs. documents:** the `desktop clean up DEc 17 2025` and `Dutch Bay material` folders contain large volumes of **dutchbay-epc-model code-repo artifacts** (audit/changelog/implementation `.md`, backup CSVs, code-upload archives) and **hash-named office exports** — not RE source documents; excluded from the themed lists above.
- **GDrive `aruna@mac.com`:** negligible — media/personal only, no RE documents.
- **Truncation caveat:** the two largest GDrive folders (650 and 358 docs) are truncated in the corpus prep (+580 and +214 unlisted names); additional EIA annexures, yield studies, and KML/KMZ layouts almost certainly exist beyond what is enumerated here. A full filename re-export would let these be cataloged.
- **Redaction:** sensitive commercial, contractual, financial and land filenames have been generalized in this public index; a full unredacted index is held privately by the project owner. Personal/non-RE items (medical, banking, vCards, personal photos, an unrelated disaster-management DSS project, census, deeds/insurance/vehicle documents) were screened out.
