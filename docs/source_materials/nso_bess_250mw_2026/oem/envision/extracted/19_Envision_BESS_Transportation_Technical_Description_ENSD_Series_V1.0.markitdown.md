1  Introduction
This document describes the transport requirements and procedures on the Battery Energy
Storage System (BESS) of Envision Energy.

To ensure the safety of operators and BESS equipment, all personnel involved in
site work must carefully read and strictly abide by safety specifications. It is the
worker’s responsibility to meet all safety, local, and general regulations that apply

for the specific site, task, or project.

For the specific safety requirements of different processes, refer to the related delivery
documentation during contract performance phase.

5.3 Vehicle and Driving Requirements
All vehicles and personnel must comply with the relevant regional laws and regulations. Envision
recommends stopping the transportation during extreme weather conditions, for example, torrential
rain, dense fog, and so on. The general requirements for transportation vehicles and driver conduct
are listed as follows:
• All vehicles must be equipped with no less than three reflective cones, two wood sleepers, and
one 3 L effective hand-held fire extinguisher.
• Vehicles must be equipped with GPS configuration during transportation (with positioning and
driving speed monitoring functions).
• All vehicle tires must be free of noticeable bulges, exposed steel wires, or sidewall cracks, and
must meet the required tread depth for safe operation.
• The maximum vehicle speed must not exceed:
­ 60 km/h on bituminous roads (Abide by the local road transportation laws and regulations
of the countries)
­ 40 km/h on cement roads
­ 30 km/h on access roads, road work, and site roads
­ 15 km/h on speed bump and curves
• Transit vehicles must submit a written report on vehicle dynamics in writing every 24 hours and
conduct regular inspections of vehicles and cargo (for example, tires, lashing, appearance of
cargo, packaging, and so on).
• The minimum height and width of the road must align with the maximum envelope profile of
transportation vehicles and cargo to maintain safety margins.
• The drivers must keep the vehicles running smoothly and avoid emergency braking or sudden
acceleration.
• Do not remove the packaging during the entire transportation process.
5.4 Road Requirements
The road requirements for transporting the BESS equipment are as follows:
• Subgrade
The straight subgrade width must be at least 4.5 m, with a compaction degree exceeding 0.94. For
problematic soil types such as soft soil and collapsible loess, the subgrade must be filled with high-
bearing capacity stones. The staggered lanes are required on the infield road, incorporating
features such as fork roads and turns.
• Pavement
The straight road surface must be at least 3.5 m wide, composed of mud gravel or mountain gravel,
with a thickness of 10 cm. Weak areas might need to receive a base treatment of 20 cm thickness.
Road flatness must be maintained within a 20-m span, and the height variation across the road
surface must not exceed 150 mm between the highest and lowest points.
• Turning radius
The turning radius is the curvature radius of the center line of the road, and the minimum turning
radius is 25 m. The detailed requirements for turning radius and road width are listed below:

• Vertical curve radius
The vertical curve must have a minimum curvature radius of 100 m, meeting the relevant road
design requirements for curve length. To accommodate changes in longitudinal slope between
sections, the vertical curve must be designed to prevent the underside of the vehicle from hitting
the road.
• Slope
Typically, the maximum longitudinal slope must not exceed 8% (4.6 degrees), but in extreme
cases, it must not surpass 14% (8 degrees). If towing is required on slopes exceeding 8%, the
owner is responsible for arranging towing services. The maximum cross slope must not exceed 3%
(2 degrees).
• Bridge
The bridge must meet the equipment transportation requirements.
5.5 Shipping Requirements
Bulk carriers are used for shipping, and the lashing methods are standard shipping lashing
methods such as twist locks and connecting rod fixings.
If container vessel transportation is adopted, the non-standard 10-foot DC BANK (Battery
Segment) and SKID bases shall be loaded into flat rack containers for shipment, while the standard
10-foot DC BANK (Combined Segment) can be coupled in pairs (connection to be completed
before ex-works) and shipped as a single 20-foot container. Cargo loaded into flat rack containers
must be properly lashed to meet both land and sea transportation requirements.

7 Equipment Temporary Storage Requirements
The temporary storage requirements for the BESS DC and AC equipment are as follows:
• Ensure that the storage surface is flat with sufficient load-bearing capacity.
• Avoid placing containers near high-temperature heat sources.

• Regularly inspect the containers and promptly report any issues to the personnel of Envision

• In the transport process, if any cargo damage is found, it must be reported promptly. Any
unauthorized repair actions are strictly prohibited.
