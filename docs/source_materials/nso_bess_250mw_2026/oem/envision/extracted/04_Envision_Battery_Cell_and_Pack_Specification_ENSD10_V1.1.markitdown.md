3.  STORAGE REQUIREMENTS

Environmental Requirements

Packs shall be stored in a clean, ventilated and cool environment to avoid direct sunlight, high temperature,
corrosive gas, severe vibration, mechanical impact and heavy pressure.

Product Protection Requirements

When the pack is stored separately before integrating to the system, the coolant inlet and outlet, positive and
negative terminals, and all other interfaces shall be protected by sealing caps to prevent dust and other foreign
particles from entering.

SOC and Voltage Difference Requirements during Storage

Pack should be regularly checked for SOC and voltage difference, whether stored separately or assembled into
the system.
