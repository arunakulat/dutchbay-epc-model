1 Introduction

Storage System (BESS).

in site work must carefully read and strictly abide by safety specifications. It is the
worker’s responsibility to meet all safety, local, and general regulations that apply
for the specific site, task, or project.
For the specific safety requirements of different processes, refer to the related delivery
documentation during contract performance phase.

6 AC System Maintenance
6.1 PCS Maintenance
6.1.1 Preparation Before Inspection
1. Only personnel who have been professionally trained and authorized by Envision are
qualified to conduct the maintenance operations. The operations must comply with the
Electrical Operating Safety Protection Manual for Energy Storage AC Integrated System.
2. Before starting maintenance, shut down the host computer. The two DC-side isolation
switches (01-QS1 and 01-QS2) and ACB (01-Q1) within the PCS are automatically
disconnected. Set maintenance switch 01-S1 to maintenance mode and remove the key.
3. Disconnect the isolation switch at the high-voltage side of the 35 kV transformer to ensure
the low-voltage side of the transformer is powered off. Simultaneously, disconnect the two
DC isolation switches of the battery main control cabinet to ensure that the PCS DC input
is powered off
4. Finally, if the auxiliary power is external power supply, it must be disconnected. If powered
by UPS, disconnect the low-voltage-side UPS of the auxiliary transformer.
5. Before operation, adhere to procedures outlined in Electrical Operating Safety Protection
Manual for Energy Storage AC Integrated System for power-off. Measure with a
multimeter to ensure safety. Wear level-4 arc flash protection suit, insulated gloves, and
safety shoes during measurement.
6. Before maintenance, use a multimeter to measure the grounding resistance of the PCS.
Ensure that the resistance between the PCS main grounding busbar and the grounding
point of the integrated system base is less than or equal to 1 Ω.
7. Ensure that the power-off period exceeds 30 minutes before equipment maintenance.
Use a multimeter to measure voltages on capacitors, copper busbars, and so on,
ensuring that they are below the safe level before continuing maintenance.
8. Clean dust with a vacuum cleaner. Avoid using blowing tools to prevent short circuits.
9. Be careful when removing the door panel during PCS maintenance to prevent injury.
10. During maintenance, prioritize attention to warning labels within the PCS, emphasizing
awareness of component hot surfaces. Certain cabinet elements, such as power
modules, capacitors, radiators, copper busbars, inductors, cables, and heat exchangers,
retain elevated temperatures for a duration after the PCS is powered off.
11. After disconnecting the DC isolation switch and ACB, residual voltages of 1500 V DC or
690 V AC persist in specific sections. For inspection and maintenance involving these
areas, including circuits related to circuit breaker incoming sides, measure the voltage
with a multimeter beforehand to ensure it is zero. During measurement, adhere to safety
protocols, wearing level-4 arc flash protection suit, insulated gloves, and safety shoes.
Follow the provided table below to disconnect the corresponding switches for

1. Before operation, power off the system and perform LOTO to prevent accidental

4. The coolant must be stored in dedicated bottles or barrels, and appropriate labels must
be affixed to their surfaces.
5. When performing pressure relief or air venting operations, take precautions against
coolant splashes and wear masks to prevent coolant from entering your mouth or nose.
6. Do not randomly adjust the valve positions that have already been set in the system.
7. Do not disassemble or assemble pipelines or valve bodies under pressure.
