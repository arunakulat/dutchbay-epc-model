## Internal inconsistencies within this document

Recorded because they are properties of the controlling source, not of the analysis:

1. **Item 20 vs item 75 — common interconnection line.** Item 20 answers **"Yes. A common grid
   interconnection line may be used"** for two projects at the selected GSS on a single land plot.
   Item 75 answers that **"only one BESS project can be connected to a single 33 kV feeder"** and that
   two projects whose nearest feasible connection point is the same feeder cannot both be
   accommodated. A common *line* into separate *feeder bays* reconciles the two only if the GSS has
   spare bays; as written the two answers point opposite ways on the same commercial question.
2. **Item 65 — unit of the Capacity Charge Rate.** The answer opens **"LKR/MW/month"** and states the
   proposed rate as **`Y LKR/MW/month`**, then gives the Applicable Capacity Charge Rate as
   **`0.15xY + (0.85xYxP2/P1)` LKR/MWh/month** — a different unit in the same answer, in the item
   whose entire purpose was to resolve unit ambiguity.
3. **Item 9 vs Addendum 01 item 01.** Item 9 states 2 months **from the Letter of Award** is
   sufficient for CEA approval; Addendum 01's revised milestone schedule sets Submission of
   Environmental Clearance at **1 month from the date of signing of the ESA**.
