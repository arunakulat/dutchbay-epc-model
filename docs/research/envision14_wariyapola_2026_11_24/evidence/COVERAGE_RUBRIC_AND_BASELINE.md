# EIA content coverage — not approvals, finance CP compliance, or EN206 design clearance

Every crosswalk row is anchored to the historical September 2025 final EIA R2 and supporting annexes: its original fifteen-turbine proposal (principally EN220/10.0; EN233 text discrepancy). This source is NOT an independently approved assessment of the proposed fourteen EN206/10.5 machines and separately developed Blue Whale T2.

## Explicit scoring criteria

For 13 conditions precedent, ratings mean only how useful the historical EIA text is to prepare a CP dossier:
- **supporting**: relevant project-specific baseline, drawings, applicant submissions, management or agency evidence; still NOT a completed condition.
- **contextual**: proposed process, preliminary indication, engineering intention or legal context without executed instrument or issuer confirmation.
- **none**: no meaningful evidence or no qualifying proof.

For 20 feasibility sections:
- **substantial**: coherent historical EIA section with project descriptions, methods, impacts, route or figures; NOT lender completeness and may be superseded.
- **partial**: some source-backed content but major verification or external studies missing.
- **insufficient**: no usable lender-feasibility evidence beyond placeholders or economic context.

The crosswalk rates source-text COVERAGE, not legal effectiveness, data freshness, IFC/Equator compliance, finance close or the revised turbine. All 13 CPs are UNVERIFIED; none is green. Historical content requires authority and engineering reconciliation.
