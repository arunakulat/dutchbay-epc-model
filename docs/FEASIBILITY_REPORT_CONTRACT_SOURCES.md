## Research controls

- Official HTML pages are used where their published summaries support the proposition.
- PDF propositions are accepted only after governed local conversion and targeted
  page/section corroboration; temporary conversion files are not project evidence and are not
  retained.
- Copyrighted standards are paraphrased. No paywalled standard text is reproduced.
- Edition, status and applicability must be rechecked when a jurisdiction or technology pack is
  implemented or assured; this ledger is dated, not self-updating.
